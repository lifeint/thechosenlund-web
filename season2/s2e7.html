Tip

Jotapata is an archaeological site in the Northern District of Israel, known as a location of the Jewish War. Here, Flavius Josephus defended the fortress against the Romans in 67 CE. Excavations since 1992 have revealed a fortified city with houses, ritual baths, and a mansion with frescoes. Findings indicate a Jewish population engaged in agriculture and crafts.

🤝 Discussion

  1. As you watched this episode, which characters or moments stood out to you the most, and why? What was it about those scenes or people that captured your attention?
  2. Atticus convinces Quintus to detain Jesus. On the way to Jotapata, he tells Gaius, “I am intrigued by your prey.” What do you think is going on in Atticus’s mind? What draws him to Jesus, and bothers him?
  3. Jesus prepares his disciples for the Sermon on the Mount. How does he explain the purpose of the sermon? Who is it intended for, and what does he hope to give those who come to hear him?
  4. Throughout the episode, we see a contrast between the disciples’ expectations of the Messiah and who Jesus actually is. How do the disciples react when Jesus is detained? What had Jesus instructed them to do, and why do you think they struggle to follow his instructions?
  5. Andrew and Philip challenge Tamar and her healed friend when they speak publicly about Jesus. They seem to have different ideas about how Jesus’ followers should respond to the growing attention and potential danger. Which perspective resonates more with you?
  6. Jesus tells his disciples that they cannot simply shut down when they are afraid. What does he teach them about facing future challenges? How should Christians respond when the future is uncertain and circumstances are beyond their control?

💭 Food for thought

  1. When Jesus is detained, the disciples panic. They blame one another, argue and consider drastic measures. How do you typically respond when you find yourself in a stressful or uncertain situation?
  2. On his return from Capernaum, Jesus takes time to pray rather than rushing straight back to the camp and the waiting disciples. What does this moment teach you about Jesus’ priorities?
  3. How do you balance the desire to share your faith with the need for wisdom and discernment?
